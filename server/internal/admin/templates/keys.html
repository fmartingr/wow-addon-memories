What the actions do

  • Revoke key stops every signed request of the key. Its characters stay on the site.
  • Revoke key and delete its characters also deletes every character of the key, with its files. Each page then gives a 404.
  • Revoke screenshots denies screenshots for the key, deletes the image files and removes the screenshot links from its characters.
