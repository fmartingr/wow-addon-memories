Memories records the journey of each of your World of Warcraft characters. WoW Journey turns that record into a page you can share.

What Memories is

Memories is a set of World of Warcraft addons for WoW Forever. It writes down what happens to each character: sessions, travel, deaths, progress, kills, gathering, loot, money, skills, groups and PvP. The data stays in the game's saved files on your computer.

The 6 addons

Each addon has its own saved file. The core addon, Memories, is always needed. You can turn each other addon off in the AddOn list to stop its recording.

What WoW Journey does

WoW Journey is a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. It reads the saved files, keeps a backup, and builds a journey for each character: a timeline, a map of the trail and your screenshots. It can also upload a character to this website, so that you can send the link to a friend.
